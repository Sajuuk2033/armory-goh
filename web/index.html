Soldiers

Change what your soldiers carry and wear

  1. Open a save from the menu above. Your original is never changed.
  2. Pick a soldier on the left.
  3. Click a slot (weapon, hat, grenades…) to swap it. Ammo follows the weapon automatically.
  4. Review & save a copy, then load the copy from the campaign menu in game.

Tip: close the game (or sit in the main menu) before saving a copy. Each soldier also has Identity (name, icon, tag), Rank and Skills sections, and a Uniform card (skin). Every section can be hidden.
