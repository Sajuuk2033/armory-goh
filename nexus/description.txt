[size=5][b]Armory - soldier editor for Gates of Hell (Dynamic Conquest)[/b][/size]

Change what your soldiers carry, wear and know, then load the result as a new campaign save.

[size=4][b]What it is[/b][/size]
Armory is a small local app (Python + your web browser). It reads your own Dynamic Conquest saves and your own game install, lets you edit soldiers, and writes a new save copy. It does not inject anything into the game and does not patch any game file. No game files are included in the download.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*] Weapons and gear: primary, sidearm, launcher, backup gun, headgear, grenades, medical, tools. Ammo follows the weapon; "Add ammo" picker; 8-wide pack grid with auto-sort and drag and drop.
[*] Uniform (skin) and rank (veterancy).
[*] Skills: weapon skill, toughness and build, anti-tank rank, specialties (stealth, medic, marksman, sniper optics, binocular optics, skilled repair, engineer).
[*] Identity: name from the game's own lists, unit-card icon, ability tag, portrait.
[*] Apply to one soldier, a whole squad, or every soldier of a role. Undo/redo, plain-language change list.
[*] A tiny generated data-only mod (armory_breeds) makes supply crates refill edited soldiers to their new kit.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*] Call to Arms - Gates of Hell: Ostfront (your own copy; developed against v1.067.0)
[*] Python 3.10 or newer (nothing else to install)
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*] Unzip anywhere.
[*] Double-click start_armory.bat (or run: python server.py). Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8791/
[*] If your game or saves are in an unusual place, open Settings and paste the folders.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Safety[/b][/size]
[list]
[*] Saves are copied, never overwritten. The copy gets its own campaign name so saving in game cannot overwrite the original.
[*] "Overwrite the original" is optional, needs confirmation and makes a backup first.
[*] Close the game before saving from Armory, and restart the game afterwards because mods are read at startup.
[*] [b]Switch on the "Armory soldier breeds" mod in the game's mod list[/b] before loading your copy. Armory creates it in the game's mods folder when you save. It carries the uniform, rank, skills, icon, tag, portrait and crate resupply for the soldiers you edited, so without it those changes will not show.
[*] Keep your own backups of saves you care about.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known limits / not verified in game[/b][/size]
[list]
[*] Verified in game with German saves: loadout, uniform, rank, skills, names, crate refill for edited soldiers.
[*] Not verified in game: portraits, unit-card icon changes, ability-tag effects, on-screen labels of generated soldier types.
[*] Soviet, British, American and Finnish saves: supported from the game's own data and unit tests, not played yet.
[*] Heavily modded campaigns may contain items Armory cannot describe.
[/list]
